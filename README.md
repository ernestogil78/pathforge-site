# PathForge

Your questing companion for World of Warcraft Forever.

PathForge helps you choose useful quest work, find known objectives and turn-ins, and spend less time on unnecessary travel.

[Get the addon on CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/pathforge)

## PathForge Companion for Windows

Download approved shared quest-location updates and optionally contribute supported findings from your adventures. The addon works without the companion or an account.

**Microsoft Store release: in preparation. No Store download is available yet.**

Cloud contributions and reports currently require a verified account and a tester invitation. Quest data is incomplete; authored 1–20 routes remain alpha.
