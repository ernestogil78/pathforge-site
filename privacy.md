# PathForge Companion privacy policy

Effective October 1, 2026. Operator: Ernesto Gil.
Contact: ernesto.gil78@gmail.com.

## Local data

The companion reads the PathForge SavedVariables file you select. It stores local
preferences, selected file paths, upload acknowledgements and saved reports in
your Windows profile. Optional remembered sign-in uses Windows encryption for a
refresh credential; the app does not save your password. The Store edition uses
a separate profile and explicit import does not copy sign-in or sharing consent.

## Optional accounts and contributions

Shared-data downloads do not require an account. Account authentication uses
Supabase and your email address. Contributions currently require an invitation,
verified account and your sharing consent. Submissions are associated with an
account identifier for access control and deduplication; they are not anonymous
to the service operator.

Supported quest facts include quest, NPC and item identifiers, in-game map
coordinates, game build, character level/class/race/faction and objective progress.
Raw objective text is replaced with a signature. Quest-fact uploads exclude chat,
character names, account-folder names and the complete SavedVariables file.
In-game coordinates are not your physical location.

## Problem reports

Reports use separate consent and can include your description, selected diagnostics
and an optional screenshot. Screenshots or text may contain personal information;
review them before sending. Reports are stored privately in Supabase, with
notification delivery through Resend to the maintainer. They are not automatically
published or used as quest-database evidence.

## Purpose and providers

Data is used to authenticate contributors, prevent duplicate or abusive submissions,
investigate problems and improve shared quest information. Supabase hosts the
PathForge service; Resend supports report notifications. Network requests can
produce provider access logs containing IP addresses and other request metadata.
Microsoft separately processes Store installation/update information under its
own privacy policy.

## Your choices and retention

You can disable sharing, sign out, remove remembered sign-in or quit the app.
Stopping uploads does not erase previously submitted information. Contact the
operator for access or deletion requests. Account deletion is not currently
self-service. Reviewed quest facts already distributed to other players cannot
be recalled from their downloaded copies merely by deleting an account.

Account-linked contributions are retained while the account is active. Access
and deletion requests are reviewed manually after verifying the requester.
Reviewed quest facts without account identifiers may remain in the shared
database; deleting an account does not recall copies already downloaded by players.

Our retention policy is to remove resolved support reports, attached screenshots
and maintainer-controlled notification email copies after 90 days from resolution.
Cleanup is a manual, reviewed operation, not an automatic expiration timer.
Unresolved reports remain available for investigation. Provider-managed logs and
backups follow the providers' retention schedules and may persist after removal
from the active service. Contact us for the status of a specific deletion request.
Local files on your computer remain under your control; server cleanup does not
remove your local reports or SavedVariables.

## Website and changes

The public website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect network request
information under its own privacy statement. The website does not add advertising
or analytics scripts. Microsoft, GitHub, Supabase and Resend process information
under their respective privacy policies. This policy will be updated here when
our practices change; the effective date identifies the current version.
